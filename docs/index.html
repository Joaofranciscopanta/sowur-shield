What's New

Everything you need for a full farm life

Core systems built and playable right now in the demo build.

🌱

Farming

  • Till → Water → Plant → Harvest cycle
  • Multi-stage crop growth
  • Seasonal calendar & day/night cycle
🐔

Animal Husbandry

  • Pet animals daily for happiness & heart particles
  • Feed via inventory or the Feeding Trough
  • Happiness (0–100) affects production output
  • Animal Roster UI tracks every animal
🎒

Inventory & Economy

  • 36-slot inventory with drag & drop
  • Automatic selling via SellBox on sleep
  • Shops & quest rewards
💬

NPCs & Story

  • Branching dialogue with persistent memory
  • Quest progression system
  • Village full of characters to meet
⚔️

Combat & World Map

  • Turn-based battles with your team
  • Explore stages on a world map
  • Assemble your party before each fight
💾

Save Slots

  • 4 slots: AutoSave + Slot 1, 2, 3
  • Play time tracked per slot
  • Auto-save on every sleep
  • 3-state Minimap (Normal / Semi / Fullscreen)

How to play

Keyboard & mouse — controller support coming later.

WASD Move E Interact Click Use tool I Inventory M Minimap 1–9 Hotbar ESC Menu / Save Shift Sprint

🚜 Farming Loop

  1. Hoe → till soil
  2. Watering Can → water it
  3. Seeds → plant & wait
  4. Left Click → harvest

🏡 Daily Routine

  1. SellBox → drag items, earn on sleep
  2. Pet & feed animals every day
  3. Talk to villagers for quests
  4. Sleep to advance to the next day
